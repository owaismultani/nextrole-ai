title: "Automate your job search"
description: "Find jobs on a schedule and have a tailored resume and cover letter drafted for each new one, ready for you to review."
---

**Settings → Automation** finds jobs while you are away and can draft a tailored resume and cover letter for each new one. You review, edit and apply yourself. Nothing is sent to an employer and no application is marked as submitted.

## Set it up

1. Connect a web search service and, for documents, an AI provider in **Settings → AI & developer**.
2. Set up discovery in **Career → Today → Schedules**: select **Add**, then **Find roles**, enter what to **Look for**, and choose **How often** (every hour, 3 hours, 6 hours or every day) and **Results per search**. An hourly schedule starts searching as soon as you save it. See [Find roles while you are busy](/guides/finding-roles-automatically).
3. Open **Settings → Automation**. Choose a **Baseline resume**, then turn on **Generate tailored resumes automatically** and/or **Generate cover letters automatically**. The two are independent, and both are off until you turn them on.
4. Select **Save automation**. A missing baseline or AI connection is explained next to the setting.

Settings → Automation shows which discovery schedule it follows and links to it. There is no second place to configure the search.

## What discovery does

Each scheduled run asks your search service for results, skips pages it already knows, and keeps open job postings. New jobs appear in your applications as **Saved**. Hourly searches ask only for recent results, with some overlap so postings the search service indexed late are still seen.

Search services rank results; they do not offer a complete feed or paging. Discovery finds relevant postings but cannot promise to list every job. The schedule's **Results per search** (5 to 20) is the most one run retrieves.

The **Discovery** section in the **Career → Pipeline** tab shows whether it is running, the last attempt, the last success, the next run, and what the last run found: queries, sources, results found, duplicates, rejected results, new matches and read failures. A run that finds nothing new is different from a run that could not search or read pages, and the panel says which. A failed run never turns discovery off.

Below the status, **Runs** lists every discovery attempt, newest first, ten at a time (use **Older** and **Newer** to page). Open a run to see its counts, what the search found and why, and the jobs it led to. Each job links to its application, the posting, and the tailored resume and cover letter made for it, so you can always tell which documents came from which run. A document you moved to the trash is no longer linked.

On the schedule, use **Run now** to search immediately, **Pause** to stop future searches and **Resume** to continue with one prompt run. Missed runs after an outage are not replayed.

## What the documents are

- **Tailored resume**: a separate copy of your baseline. The summary and experience wording are adjusted for the job and skills are reordered. Dates, employers, titles and education are untouched. A rewrite that adds a figure your resume never had is discarded and the original wording kept, with a note.
- **Cover letter**: written from the job, your baseline resume and facts you have approved. When the posting or your resume does not say something a good letter needs, it is listed as something to fill in rather than invented.

Both are linked to the application. **Ready for review** means the documents were made. It does not mean you approved them, and it is separate from your application's status.

## Daily limit

**Documents per day** caps how many documents are generated. Each resume or letter counts once, on its first attempt; retries do not count again. The count resets at midnight in your timezone. Work over the limit waits and shows when it becomes eligible again.

## Turning things off and on

- Turning a document type off stops new work of that type from starting, including work already waiting. A document being written finishes and is saved. Canceled work stays canceled when you turn it back on.
- Turning a type on affects jobs found from then on. Jobs you already have are never processed in bulk. Choose **Prepare documents** on a role in Today to do that for one job.
- Pausing or turning off the discovery schedule stops future searches. Jobs and documents you have stay, and document preparation for jobs already found continues while its toggle is on.

## Review, edit and regenerate

In **Career → Pipeline**, the **Document preparation** section lists each job with its status, five at a time, and can be filtered to **Needs attention** or **Ready for review**. **Career → Today** shows a one-line **Pipeline** summary with those counts and links here. Each cover letter lists what it could not say from your resume under **N gaps to fill**, collapsed until you open it. Settings → Automation links to it. Open a document to edit or download it in the editor. **Retry** repeats a step that needs attention. **Regenerate** makes a new document from the current baseline and posting; the earlier one, including your edits, is left alone. A document is flagged when your baseline or the posting changed after it was made. Rediscovering a job never regenerates its documents.

## See the whole picture

**Overview** (first in the sidebar on the web; **Career → Overview** in the mobile app) brings the search together for the last 7, 30 or 90 days:

- **Needs you now**: documents ready for review or needing attention, interviews in the next 7 days, reminders and briefings, new roles to review, and a warning when discovery is failing. Each links to where you act.
- **Pipeline**: how many jobs were found, saved, prepared, applied to, screened, interviewed for and offered in the window. Each step counts jobs that reached it in the window, so later steps can include jobs found earlier.
- **Discovery**: state, last success, next run, a bar per run (new jobs found, red when it failed) and totals. It says when the last five runs found nothing new but kept seeing jobs you already have, which means scheduling is working and the search itself needs changing.
- **Document automation**: documents generated today against your daily limit, when it resets, work waiting for the limit, and documents that are out of date.
- **Interviews**: your next interviews.

For response rates, time to first reply and tailored versus base resume results, see **Applications → Insights**.
