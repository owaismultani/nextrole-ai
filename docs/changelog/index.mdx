NextRole AI is built on [Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume) v6. Changes to the resume
builder before NextRole AI are in [Reactive Resume's changelog](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume/blob/main/docs/changelog/index.mdx).

## October 27, 2026

- **A new landing page.** NextRole AI is presented as a career team of six specialists, with a three.js particle field
  that reshapes for each part of the job search and a 60-second tour recorded from the real app.
- **Job sources in discovery.** Discovery searches keyless job APIs (Bundesagentur, Himalayas, Jobicy, The Muse,
  Arbeitnow, Remote OK, We Work Remotely) and followed company boards alongside web search. Keyed sources (Adzuna, Reed,
  Jooble, USAJOBS) use shared or personal keys. Runs record what each source found.

## October 22, 2026

- **Compare and steer regenerations.** Compare any two versions of a document, and give a regenerate a focus such as
  "lead with the onboarding work".
- **Regenerate in place.** A regenerate writes over the same document and keeps the earlier generation in History.
  Custom writing instructions are honored on every generation.
- **Overview** brings notifications, your next actions, upcoming interviews and automation status into one home page,
  replacing Career.
- **Documents by ownership**: see your baseline, documents for each application, and reusable documents apart.
- **Application workspace by purpose**, with offers added from the application itself.
- **Navigation** reorganized into Overview, Discover, Applications, Documents, Knowledge, Coach and Automation, with
  Overview on the phone's bottom bar.
- **Pipeline transparency**: each of the eight steps from schedule to "You apply" says what it is doing and why.
- **Guided setup** for new accounts: confirm a baseline resume against a checklist and ATS score, connect services,
  preview a search and start discovery.

## October 21, 2026

- **Overview dashboard** with what needs you, application outcomes and the latest discovery.
- **Automation prompts**: edit the additional instructions that guide tailored resumes and cover letters.

## October 20, 2026

- **Automatic document preparation.** For each new role discovery finds, NextRole AI can draft a tailored resume and a
  cover letter from your baseline, within a daily limit you set. Nothing is sent to an employer.

## October 19, 2026

- **Recurring, observable discovery.** Search for roles every hour, three hours, six hours or day, and review every run:
  what it searched, what it found, what it skipped and why.
