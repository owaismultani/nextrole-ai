NextRole AI is a free resume builder. You get every feature with no premium tier, no watermark and no paywall on downloads. The whole project is open source, so nothing is held back.

## What you get for free

- **Unlimited documents.** Keep as many resumes and cover letters as you need, and copy one for each job.
- **18 templates.** Switch at any time without retyping; your content stays the same and only the design changes.
- **Design controls.** Font pairings (or any of about 500 fonts), text size, density, accent colour, margins, A4, Letter or Free-form pages, and multi-page layouts.
- **Every download format.** PDF, Word, OpenDocument, Markdown and JSON, as often as you like.
- **Check mode.** A readability score, issues pinned to the exact lines on your page, and keyword matching against a job posting.
- **Public links.** Share a live page with an optional password and see how often it is viewed.
- **Application tracker.** Follow every job from saved to offer, with a board, a calendar for interviews and insights.
- **Version history.** Name versions and go back to any earlier state.
- **Imports.** Bring in a PDF, a NextRole AI or JSON Resume file, or a LinkedIn data export. Word files work too once you connect an AI provider.
- **50+ languages** for the app interface.

AI features are free to use in NextRole AI too, but they run on your own AI provider account, which may charge you. See [AI resume builder](/use-cases/ai-resume-builder).

## Where it runs

NextRole AI has no central hosted service: you run it yourself, or use an instance someone runs for you, such as your school or company. One Docker image and a PostgreSQL database are enough; see [Self-hosted resume builder](/use-cases/self-hosted-resume-builder). You need an account on the instance so your documents are saved between visits.

If you only want to check an existing PDF, you do not need an account at all: the [ATS checker](/guides/using-the-ats-checker) runs in your browser.
