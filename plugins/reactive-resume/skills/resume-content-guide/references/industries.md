## 1. How to use these playbooks

Each playbook gives the section order, the must-haves, the signature metrics, what to remove, and the length. Academia, executives and military transition are in situations.md.

Evidence varies. Playbooks marked `convention, not verified` (data science, product, project management, marketing, teaching, trades, hospitality and retail, nonprofit) had no primary source in the research for this guide; present them as defaults the user can override. Metrics named below are the _kind_ of number readers look for. Ask the user for their real figures; never fill one in.

## 2. Software and tech

`convention`, with vendor and university sources.

- **Order:** Header (with GitHub or portfolio) → Summary (experienced only) → Experience → Projects (above Experience for new grads) → Skills (grouped) → Education (first for students).
- **Must-haves:** shipped systems; scope (users, requests per second, data volume, cost); ownership; the technology named inside the bullets. A Skills line grouped as Languages / Frameworks / Cloud and infrastructure / Tools, listing only what the candidate can defend in an interview.
- **Bullet shape:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]", a formula attributed to Google recruiters (`convention`). Hand rewriting to resume-bullet-writer when available.
- **Projects:** 2–3 substantial projects beat six weak ones. Format each as name, GitHub, live demo, then stack, then outcome.
- **GitHub:** GitHub's own guide suggests pinning 3–5 varied, job-relevant repositories with READMEs (`survey`, vendor documentation). A link helps but doesn't replace strong bullets.
- **Remove:** skill bars, coursework once experienced, every library ever touched.
- **Length:** 1 page for students (Google recruiters told a 2025 student workshop: one page, PDF, bullets); 1–2 when experienced.

## 3. Data science and machine learning

`convention, not verified`.

As software, plus: methods used (experimentation, causal inference, model types), data scale, and the _business_ outcome (revenue, cost, a decision changed). Publications only for research roles. Kaggle only with a notable rank.

## 4. Product management

`convention, not verified`.

Lead with outcomes (adoption, retention, revenue, satisfaction scores), launches with scope, cross-functional leadership (team size, functions involved) and discovery or experimentation work. Avoid feature lists without results. Not the same as project management (section 4a).

## 4a. Project and programme management

`convention`, not verified.

- **Order:** Summary naming the project-management target → Experience → Certifications → Education.
- **Must-haves:** for each project, its scope, budget, schedule, team or stakeholder count, the delivery method only if the posting names it, and the outcome against the plan (on time, or late by N weeks; budget variance; risks retired). Every figure comes from the user.
- **Certifications with exact status:** "PMP (PMI), 2026", "PMP candidate, exam scheduled 03/2027", "CAPM", "PRINCE2 Foundation". Never "PMP-trained" when it means not certified. PRINCE2 is common in UK, Gulf and Australian postings and PMP in US and Gulf postings; mirror the posting.
- **Not product management:** no adoption or retention metrics unless the user owned a product.
- **Metric ideas:** the resume-bullet-writer skill's metric library (project and program management), if available.

## 5. Design, UX and creative

`survey`: a Nielsen Norman Group survey of 204 UX hiring managers.

- Portfolio link at the top. 3–5 case studies, each covering the problem, the user's role and collaborators, process (research, sketches), the solution, challenges and rejected concepts, impact and lessons. Make them scannable, show the kind of work the user wants next, and redact NDA work.
- The resume itself stays single-column and parseable; visual flair lives in the portfolio.

## 6. Marketing

`convention, not verified`.

Channel metrics (acquisition cost, return on ad spend, conversion, pipeline sourced or influenced), budget managed, audience growth, the tools the posting names (analytics, CRM, automation platforms), and a portfolio link for content or brand roles.

## 7. Sales

`convention`, vendor sources.

- **The first number a reader sees:** quota attainment as a percentage and an amount, per year. Add rank ("#3 of 18 account executives") and President's Club with the year; many companies award it to a large share of reps, so rank gives it context.
- **Then:** average deal size or annual contract value, sales cycle, segment (SMB, mid-market, enterprise), new logos versus expansion, and a methodology (MEDDIC, Challenger) only if the posting names it.
- **SDR and BDR:** pipeline generated, meetings set, conversion.
- **Sales managers:** team attainment versus personal, headcount, ramp time, retention.
- **Honesty:** use percentages when amounts are confidential. Show the strongest years only with honest context; never present a hidden bad year as continuous over-attainment.

## 8. Investment banking, private equity, finance

`convention`, third-party guides.

- **Length:** one page under about 10 years of experience.
- **Order:** Education first for students and roughly the first 3 years, with GPA and test scores where strong.
- **Deals:** transaction type, sector, size, the user's role, outcome. Experienced hires add a deal sheet as an addendum.
- **Credentials:** licences (Series 7, 63, 79), CFA status stated precisely ("Passed Level II of the CFA Program"; the CFA Institute restricts how its marks are used, so verify the wording against its guidance), tools (Bloomberg, CapIQ, FactSet), modelling.
- **Interests:** a short line is conventional and used as an icebreaker.

## 9. Management consulting

Criteria from McKinsey's own careers blog; format is `convention`.

- McKinsey says it looks for problem solving, inclusive leadership and entrepreneurial drive, wants what the candidate accomplished and why it mattered, quantified, and counts non-business experience (internships, volunteering, part-time jobs, military).
- **Format:** one page, single column, 3–5 quantified bullets per role. Education first for students and recent graduates.

## 10. Healthcare and nursing

University career offices and vendor guides.

- **Licence near the top:** RN licence, issuing state, multistate compact status. Before licensure: "Eligible for RN licensure, [Month YYYY]".
- **Certifications:** spelled out, then abbreviation, issuer and expiry: "Basic Life Support (BLS), American Heart Association, exp. 06/2027".
- **Clinical rotations:** for new grads, list as experience with facility, unit type and hours. Describe workload in aggregate only; patient-identifying detail is disqualifying.
- **Electronic health records:** name the systems by brand and mirror the posting's wording.
- **Length:** 1 page for new grads; 1–2 when experienced.
- **Format:** no tables, text boxes, images, columns, or contact details in the page header (University of Maryland nursing tip sheet). Achievements over responsibilities (University of Michigan nursing).
- Physicians and researchers use a CV (situations.md, academic CV).

## 11. Teaching (K-12)

`convention, not verified`.

Certification or licence with state, subject and grade-level endorsements and expiry; student teaching listed as experience; measurable outcomes (assessment growth, pass rates); differentiation and accommodations work; curriculum built; learning platforms and ed-tech used; extracurriculars coached. Background clearances only if the posting asks.

## 12. Legal

Law-school career offices.

- **Students:** one page. Heading → Education (honours; law journal listed first, then moot court and trial team) → Experience → optional Interests, Languages, Skills.
- **Bar admission section:** "BAR ADMISSION — State of Ohio, Expected November 2025". Admitted attorneys list jurisdictions and years, plus federal court admissions.
- **Bullets:** name the work product (memoranda, dispositive motions) and the matter type. Lateral hires add a representative-matters list, conventionally as an addendum.
- **Gaps:** law-school offices advise addressing a gap or a changed graduation date proactively.
- A law-school career office estimates legal employers spend 10–30 seconds on a first review (`convention`, Chicago-Kent guide; no study), so keep student resumes to one page.

## 13. US federal (Title 5, USAJOBS)

`official`: OPM guidance, changed September 2025. Verify on the specific job announcement.

- **Two-page hard limit since 27 September 2025.** A resume over two pages is ineligible if it is the only one submitted. Covers competitive and excepted service, internal and external.
- **Exceptions:** non-Title 5, judicial and legislative-branch employers may allow longer documents; medical and research roles needing a CV attach it under "other documents".
- **Per job:** title, employer, series and grade for federal jobs, start and end as MM/YYYY, hours per week.
- **Education:** institution, completion date, degree type, GPA. Licences and certifications when required.
- **Optional when relevant:** current clearance, job-related training, languages, affiliations, publications, special-programme eligibility (for example Schedule A, with the letter submitted separately).
- **Language:** align wording with the announcement's qualifications, truthfully.
- **Format suggested by OPM:** PDF, sans-serif font, 0.5-inch margins, 10 pt body, 14 pt titles, file under 5 MB.
- **Essays:** Merit Hiring Plan vacancies at GS-05 and above reportedly add short essay questions outside the resume (secondary source; out of this skill's scope).
- **Before → after:** "Program Analyst, Dept of X, 2021–2024" → "Program Analyst, GS-0343-11, Department of X | 05/2021–08/2024 | 40 hrs/week".

## 14. Skilled trades

`convention, not verified`.

Licences (type, state, class, expiry), certifications, equipment and tools operated (makes and models where the posting names them), apprenticeship and journeyman hours, CDL class and endorsements, and a safety record if the user has one ("0 recordables across [n] hours"). OSHA 10 and 30 Outreach cards are course-completion cards and OSHA says it doesn't "certify" workers (verify at osha.gov), so write "OSHA 30-Hour Construction (Outreach card)". High school or GED can stay.

## 15. Hospitality and retail

`convention, not verified`.

Volume (covers per shift, sales, transactions), customer metrics (ratings, mystery-shop scores), point-of-sale systems, food-safety and alcohol-service certifications with expiry, languages, promotions. Availability goes in the application form, not the resume, unless the posting asks.

## 16. Nonprofit

`convention, not verified`.

Mission outcomes (people served, programme results), funds raised or grants won (amount, funder type), volunteers managed, budget owned. Volunteer roles count as experience.

## 17. Applicant-tracking formatting: what is documented

**Vendor documentation** (`survey`):

- **Greenhouse:** parsing fails or partly fails with graphics, photos or word art; tables, headers and footers; columns; letter-spaced text; image-only files; files over 2.5 MB; placeholder data. Use full job titles and company names, and keep name and contact details in the body. A failed parse attaches the file; it does not reject the candidate.
- **Lever:** parses Word, PDF, RTF, HTML and ODT, not JPG or PNG. Self-test: if you can't highlight the text, it isn't parseable.
- **Textkernel** (a parser built into many systems): column CVs are at least 10–15% of its traffic; after a model upgrade about 90% render well, which still leaves about 10% mixed up.
- **US OPM:** PDF recommended, maximum 5 MB.
- **Workday:** no public candidate-side parsing documentation found. Claims such as "Workday drops the right column" come from third-party blogs; don't present them as fact.

**Rules for the skill:**

1. One column for any portal submission. A two-column design is fine for a copy handed to a person (referral, email).
2. Name, email, phone and links in the body, not in a header, footer or text box.
3. No tables or text boxes for layout; skills as plain lines.
4. Standard headings: Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Licences. No "My Journey".
5. Don't rely on icons as labels.
6. A text-based PDF unless the posting asks for Word. Check: select-all and paste gives the same text in order; under 2.5 MB; no hidden text.
7. One date format throughout; month and year.

**What actually filters people:** knockout questions and criteria recruiters configure. In a Harvard Business School and Accenture survey, most employers said qualified candidates were screened out for not matching criteria exactly, and secondary coverage reports that about half filtered out resumes with gaps over six months (`survey`, secondhand; the exact figure is unverified). The practical answer: use the posting's exact requirement wording where it's true, state licences and certifications explicitly, and account for gaps. Keyword strategy belongs to resume-tailor.

**Hidden text:** measured prevalence in 2025 ranges from about 1% (`survey`: Greenhouse estimate; `study`: Duke-led, about 197,000 hireEZ resumes) to about 10% (`survey`: ManpowerGroup's AI scans). Parsers extract it, recruiters see it, and some employers reject for it. Some templates and generators insert it, so check every audited resume.

## 18. LinkedIn and resume consistency

`convention, not verified`.

- **Must match:** employer names, job titles (or the official title with the functional title in parentheses, on both) and start and end month and year. Background checks verify these, and mismatches read as fabrication.
- **May differ:** LinkedIn can hold more roles, longer descriptions, media and recommendations; the resume is the tailored subset.
- **Photo:** a LinkedIn headshot is normal even where the resume omits one.
- **Gaps:** label them the same way on both. LinkedIn offers a career-break entry type (verify in the product).
- Update LinkedIn before applying.

## 19. Question bank by industry

- **Sales:** quota and attainment for each of the last 3 years? Rank and team size? Clubs or awards, and which year? Deal size, cycle length, segment?
- **Nursing:** licence state, compact status, expiry? Certifications with issuer and expiry? Units and hours per rotation? Which health-record systems and modules?
- **Federal:** series and grade? Exact MM/YYYY dates? Hours per week? Clearance level and status?
- **Finance:** deals (type, size, sector, your role)? Licences? CFA level passed?
- **Legal:** bar jurisdictions and year, or pending? Journal or moot court role? Writing samples?
- **Tech:** what scale (users, requests, data)? What changed because of your work? Which stack, used where?
- **Design:** portfolio URL? Which 3–5 projects map to this role? NDA constraints?
- **Trades:** licence types and classes? Equipment operated? Hours logged? Safety record?
- **Project management:** largest project by budget, people and duration? Delivered on time and on budget? Certifications and their status?
- **Military:** occupation code? People, budget and equipment led? Clearance?
