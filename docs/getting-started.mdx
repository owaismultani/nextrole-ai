NextRole AI is a free and open-source job search app. It finds roles on a schedule you set, drafts a tailored resume
and cover letter for each one it keeps, prepares you for the interview and helps you compare offers. It prepares
everything and stops there: nothing is submitted or sent in your name, and AI features run on your own provider key.

Underneath is a full resume builder. NextRole AI is built on [Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume),
so you also get its editor, templates, Check mode, sharing and exports.

## Where it runs

NextRole AI has no central hosted service. You run it yourself with Docker, or use an instance someone runs for you,
such as your school or company. It works on phones, tablets and computers, in 55 languages. These guides use
`nextrole.example.com` as the site's address; replace it with yours.

## Your data

Your work belongs to you. Download any document as JSON and import it back later, export everything in your account
from **Settings**, and delete your account at any time. The source code is public on
[GitHub](https://github.com/owaismultani/nextrole-ai) under the MIT license, so anyone can check what the app does with your data.
