## 1. Evidence tags

| Tag          | Means                                                                                                                                  | How to present it                                 |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `official`   | Statute, regulator, or a government careers service (EEOC, OPM, UK National Careers Service, Job Bank Canada, Japan's labour ministry) | As the rule for that jurisdiction, date-stamped   |
| `study`      | Field experiment or peer-reviewed research                                                                                             | As measured evidence, with its setting and limits |
| `survey`     | Employer survey, vendor documentation, commercial study                                                                                | As indicative; name who ran it                    |
| `convention` | Practitioner consensus                                                                                                                 | As a default the user can override                |

Most resume advice is `convention`. Government careers services are the most reliable source for country norms but carry little evidence on what actually works.

## 2. What the research shows

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Tag                                                                   | Use it for                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| In 2012, 30 recruiters spent about 80% of an initial look on name, current and previous title and company, their dates, and education. A 2018 rerun reported 7.4 seconds for the first glance.                                             | `survey` (vendor, small, thin methods)                                | Making titles, employers, dates and education easy to find; against functional layouts. Not for "6 seconds" claims |
| Callbacks fall steeply over the first ~8 months of the current unemployment spell, then level off; the drop is larger in tight labour markets (12,054 resumes).                                                                            | `study`                                                               | Prioritising a current gap; applying quickly after a layoff                                                        |
| Parents with a family "opt-out" gap got about 5% callbacks, against about 9% for unemployed and about 15% for continuously employed parents; mothers and fathers alike. In a survey experiment, fathers were rated worse on some measures. | `study` (ASR 2018)                                                    | The trade-off when labelling a caregiving gap                                                                      |
| Disclosing a disability cut employer interest by about 26%, mostly among experienced applicants and small firms not covered by the ADA (6,000+ applications).                                                                              | `study`                                                               | Disability disclosure is the user's choice                                                                         |
| Age discrimination in hiring, strongest against older women (40,000+ applications varying graduation dates).                                                                                                                               | `study` (JPE 2019)                                                    | Old graduation years; trimming early career                                                                        |
| Black-coded names got 2.1 percentage points fewer contacts; the worst 20% of firms produced about half the gap (83,000 applications, 108 large firms).                                                                                     | `study` (QJE 2022)                                                    | Name questions, only when the user raises them                                                                     |
| "Whitened" resumes from Black and Asian applicants got more than twice the callbacks, even at employers advertising diversity.                                                                                                             | `study` (ASQ 2016)                                                    | Same                                                                                                               |
| Germany, same photo model: German name 18.8% callbacks, Turkish name 13.5%, Turkish name with headscarf 4.2%.                                                                                                                              | `study`                                                               | Photo decisions in German-speaking markets                                                                         |
| Israel, 5,312 CVs: attractive men gained from a photo; women did best with no photo.                                                                                                                                                       | `study`                                                               | Photo decisions generally                                                                                          |
| Upper-class hobbies helped men's callbacks at law firms but not women's.                                                                                                                                                                   | `study` (ASR 2016)                                                    | Interests section                                                                                                  |
| They/them pronouns lowered positive responses by about 5 percentage points against no pronouns and about 4 against he/him or she/her.                                                                                                      | `study` (preregistered 2023; draft results, preliminary)              | Pronoun decisions                                                                                                  |
| Ban-the-box laws widened the white–Black callback gap at affected firms, consistent with employers guessing once records are hidden.                                                                                                       | `study` (QJE)                                                         | Context for criminal-record questions                                                                              |
| Anonymised applications in a German pilot (8,550 applications) raised invitations for women and migrants; employers self-selected into the pilot.                                                                                          | `study`                                                               | Why minimal personal data is the default                                                                           |
| Fewer than 40% of employers screen new graduates by GPA, for the third year running; about two-thirds use skills-based hiring for entry level (n=216).                                                                                     | `survey` (NACE 2025)                                                  | GPA rule; evidence in bullets over lists                                                                           |
| Two-page resumes were picked more often (2.3x overall; 1.4x entry level, 2.6x mid, 2.9x managerial) in a 482-reviewer simulation.                                                                                                          | `survey` (vendor, unpublished significance)                           | Against rigid one-page rules, not as proof                                                                         |
| Parsing fails with columns, tables, headers and footers, images; a failed parse attaches the file rather than rejecting it.                                                                                                                | `survey` (Greenhouse documentation)                                   | Layout advice                                                                                                      |
| Measured prevalence of hidden text or AI-directed prompts in 2025 resumes ranges from about 1% (Greenhouse; a Duke-led study of about 197,000 hireEZ resumes) to about 10% (ManpowerGroup's AI scans).                                     | `survey` (Greenhouse, ManpowerGroup); `study` (Duke-led, hireEZ data) | Why audits check for hidden text                                                                                   |

## 3. Myths and dubious statistics

Never repeat these as fact. When a user quotes one, give the short correction.

| Claim                                                                                     | Correction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Recruiters spend 6 (or 7.4) seconds on a resume."                                        | Vendor eye-tracking studies of about 30 recruiters, not peer-reviewed, timing only the first keep-reading decision. A later self-report survey (Kickresume, about 120 HR respondents) had 76% saying they spend 30+ seconds. Neither is robust. |
| "75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human sees them."                            | Traced to marketing by a defunct resume-optimisation vendor around 2012, with no published method. Failed parses usually attach the file; filtering comes from recruiter-set criteria.                                                          |
| "Two-page resumes are 2.3x more likely to be chosen."                                     | A vendor simulation, not hiring outcomes. Weak evidence against one-page dogma, not proof.                                                                                                                                                      |
| "One page is a hard rule."                                                                | No rigorous evidence. UK and Canadian guides allow 2 pages, Australian public sector up to 4; academic CVs are unlimited.                                                                                                                       |
| "Federal resumes should be 3–5 pages."                                                    | Outdated: since 27 September 2025 Title 5 resumes over 2 pages are ineligible.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| "USAJOBS doesn't enforce the page limit" / "federal HR doesn't index PDFs."               | Blog claims contradicted by, or absent from, OPM guidance.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| "GPA 3.0 is a universal cut-off."                                                         | No source supports a universal threshold. The 3.0 / 3.5 convention is practitioner consensus.                                                                                                                                                   |
| "GPA screening fell from 75% to 46%."                                                     | A secondary figure that conflicts with NACE's own numbers. Cite NACE directly.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| "79% of hiring managers would hire someone with an explained gap (LinkedIn)."             | Not traceable to LinkedIn's published survey.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| "41% of employers say gaps hurt hireability."                                             | From a garbled vendor page; original survey not found.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| "Hiring systems automatically reject 6+ month gaps."                                      | The Hidden Workers report confirms systems exclude caregivers and others, but the specific figure is secondhand and unverified.                                                                                                                 |
| "88% of employers' ATS reject qualified candidates."                                      | Misquoted: it is employers' survey answer that qualified people are screened out for inexact matches, not a measured rejection rate.                                                                                                            |
| "82% of hiring managers prefer volunteer experience."                                     | Widely repeated; method not verified.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| "Half of German recruiters want a photo and 1 in 5 reject applications without one."      | The original survey couldn't be located.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| "A photo is mandatory on a German CV."                                                    | False since the AGG (2006). Customary, optional, and risky for employers who screen on it.                                                                                                                                                      |
| "A German Lebenslauf must be signed."                                                     | Not required; still expected at banks, insurers, the public sector and on paper.                                                                                                                                                                |
| "Italian CVs legally need the GDPR consent line."                                         | Italian privacy law says consent for CV data is not due; the clause survives as custom.                                                                                                                                                         |
| "Polish CVs legally need a RODO clause."                                                  | Not for the standard data the Labour Code lists; useful only for extra data or staying on file.                                                                                                                                                 |
| "France requires anonymous CVs."                                                          | The 2006 provision never took effect and was removed in 2015.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| "Europass is the required EU format."                                                     | Voluntary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| "Japanese rirekisho must state gender."                                                   | Optional on the 2021 labour-ministry template.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| "South Korea bans resume photos."                                                         | Only public-sector blind hiring does; private-sector employers with 30+ staff may not request appearance details. A private-sector photo ban was proposed in 2024, not confirmed.                                                               |
| "Singapore's Workplace Fairness Act is in force."                                         | Passed; commencement targeted for end-2027.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| "75% of Gulf recruiters want a photo."                                                    | A misquoted 2018 poll of mostly job seekers about fresh graduates; a 2013 poll said 54%. Neither measured recruiters.                                                                                                                           |
| "It's illegal for a US candidate to include a photo or birth date."                       | US law restricts employers asking. Candidates may volunteer them, against the norm and with bias risk.                                                                                                                                          |
| "Functional resumes are auto-rejected by ATS."                                            | No vendor documentation or study. Recruiter distrust is practitioner consensus.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| "Single-column resumes parse at 93% vs 86%."                                              | No traceable method.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| "Parsers read columns cleanly now (62% → 90%)."                                           | One vendor's improvement to its own engine, still leaving about 10% mis-rendered; other vendors still list columns as a failure cause.                                                                                                          |
| "Workday drops the right column."                                                         | Third-party blogs only; no Workday documentation found.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| "41% of job seekers hide secret text."                                                    | Vendor self-report. Measured prevalence ranges from about 1% (Greenhouse; a Duke-led study of ~197,000 hireEZ resumes) to about 10% (ManpowerGroup's AI scans).                                                                                 |
| "White-font keywords or hidden prompts beat the ATS."                                     | Anecdote only. Hidden text surfaces in parsed text, vendors detect it, some employers reject for it.                                                                                                                                            |
| "Objective statements are an instant reject."                                             | Opinion. UK guidance still recommends a short targeted introduction.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Portfolio statistics ("87% of hiring managers value portfolios over resumes" and similar) | No traceable primary source.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| "Ontario added Canadian-experience rules to its Human Rights Code."                       | It is a 2013 Commission policy, persuasive but not law.                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## 4. Contested advice

Present both sides and the default; the user decides.

| Topic                                     | Default                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| References line                           | Omit in the US; optional line in the UK (still recommended officially); referees common in Australia and NZ |
| One page or two                           | Tie to career stage: 1 under ~5 years in US-style markets, otherwise up to 2                                |
| Photo in German-speaking countries        | Ask; include for traditional employers on opt-in; omit for tech, international and English-language ads     |
| Removing old graduation dates             | The user's call: it lowers an age signal but a missing date can itself signal age                           |
| Pronouns, chosen-name signals, visa lines | Present the evidence; the user decides                                                                      |
| Years-only dates to blur short gaps       | Prefer month/year plus a labelled break; years-only conflicts with federal rules and background checks      |
| Functional format after incarceration     | Prefer hybrid; re-entry guides suggest functional, recruiters distrust it                                   |
| Naming a caregiving gap                   | Name it honestly; give the opt-out evidence; never fabricate employment to cover it                         |

## 5. How to answer "is that true?"

1. Find the claim in sections 2 or 3.
2. If it's a myth, say so in one sentence and give what is known instead.
3. If it's real, give the setting (country, sample, year) and the tag; don't stretch a single study into a universal rule.
4. If it isn't here, say you can't verify it, and treat it as `convention` at best. Don't invent a source.
